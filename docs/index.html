Turbofan Fleet — Predictive Maintenance

Which of these 100 jet engines is about to fail, and which do I fix first?

The problem. Jet engines don't fail on a schedule. In this fleet they last anywhere from 128 to 362 cycles. Replace them all early and you burn money; wait too long and one fails in flight.

The result. This system reads 12 sensors, predicts how many cycles each engine has left, and turns that into a maintenance decision. Tested by replaying 100 engines it had never seen against the true failure times — it caught every engine that was going to fail, and gave the crew a median of 40 cycles of warning.
