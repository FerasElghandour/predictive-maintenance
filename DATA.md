# Data and evaluation

Source: [NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence data repository](https://www.nasa.gov/content/prognostics-center-of-excellence-data-set-repository), C-MAPSS FD001 simulated turbofan degradation records. The existing raw benchmark files are retained; the corrected evaluation fingerprints each input.

The 100 training engines run to failure. The 100 test trajectories stop before failure and are therefore right-censored for eventual failure coverage. Their endpoint RUL values support benchmark scoring but do not establish an operational safety outcome. No client sensor records are included.
